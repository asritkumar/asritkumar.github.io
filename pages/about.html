Biography

Asritkumar S is an independent digital creator and actor who is actively building a footprint in the Tamil digital entertainment and independent cinema space. Based out of the Chennai and Navalur region in Tamil Nadu, he takes on a multifaceted approach to his creative projects, regularly handling writing, acting, and directing duties. As an emerging young talent, his work leans heavily into narrative digital storytelling and experimental concepts, which he distributes directly to his growing audience online.

Working across multiple creative roles, he explores acting, writing, direction and independent film production. His projects reflect his interest in storytelling, cinema and digital filmmaking.

He is best known for creating, writing, and starring in the Tamil short film titled "Thalaiyila Poduvom Dove'u Poiyee Duchu Da En Love'u", a project that showcases his interest in unique storytelling devices like time-loop mechanics.

He collaborates frequently with a close-knit circle of local creators, including co-director Sai Sharvesh, composer Anishkumar S, and visual effects editor Yashwanth. He organizes and shares all of his creative portfolios, behind-the-scenes updates, and short videos across his primary social media platforms, including his official YouTube channel Asritkumar S and his Instagram handle @asritkumar_official.
