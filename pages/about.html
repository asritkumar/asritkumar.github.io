Biography

Asritkumar S is an emerging actor and filmmaker interested in developing original cinematic stories and bringing creative ideas to the screen.

Working across multiple creative roles, he explores acting, writing, direction and independent film production. His projects reflect his interest in storytelling, cinema and digital filmmaking.

Through ASRIT FILMS, he is developing a collection of cinematic projects and building a creative identity in independent filmmaking.

His filmography includes the Tamil project "Thalaiyila Poduvom Dove'u Poiyee Duchu Da En Love'u", for which a trailer has been released. His upcoming projects include further independent cinematic productions.
